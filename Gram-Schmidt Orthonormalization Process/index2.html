Gram-Schmidt Orthonormalization

Master the mathematical foundation of digital communication systems. Transform linearly independent signals into orthonormal basis functions used in OFDM, MIMO, and signal space analysis.

Mathematical Foundation

What is Gram-Schmidt?

The Gram-Schmidt process is a fundamental algorithm in digital communication used to convert a set of linearly independent signals into an orthonormal basis. This is crucial in signal space representation and optimal receiver design.

Applications

  • OFDM Systems: Creating orthogonal subcarriers for WiFi and LTE
  • MIMO Processing: Spatial signal separation and beamforming
  • Signal Space Analysis: Optimal receiver design
  • Compression: Transform coding and basis selection

The Algorithm

Step 1: First basis function

φ₁(t) = s₁(t) / ||s₁(t)||

Step 2: Project and subtract

cij = ⟨si(t), φj(t)⟩

gi(t) = si(t) - Σ cijφj(t)

Step 3: Normalize

φi(t) = gi(t) / ||gi(t)||
