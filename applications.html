Real-World Applications

Discover how digital communication and signal processing concepts power the technology around us every day. From your smartphone to satellite communications, these principles are everywhere.

Cellular & Mobile Communications

4G/LTE Networks

Modern cellular networks use advanced modulation schemes and signal processing techniques for high-speed data transmission.

  • OFDM Modulation: Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing for spectrum efficiency
  • MIMO Technology: Multiple antennas for increased capacity
  • Adaptive Modulation: QPSK to 64-QAM based on channel conditions

5G New Radio (NR)

Next-generation wireless technology pushing the boundaries of communication systems.

  • Massive MIMO: Up to 256 antenna elements
  • Beamforming: Directed signal transmission
  • 256-QAM: High-order modulation for ultra-high speeds

WiFi & Wireless LANs

WiFi 6 (802.11ax)

Latest WiFi standard with enhanced performance

  • • OFDMA for multiple users
  • • 1024-QAM modulation
  • • Up to 9.6 Gbps throughput
  • • Target Wake Time (TWT)

WiFi 5 (802.11ac)

Widely deployed gigabit WiFi

  • • 256-QAM modulation
  • • 80/160 MHz channels
  • • MU-MIMO support
  • • Up to 3.5 Gbps

Legacy Standards

Foundation technologies

  • • 802.11n: MIMO introduction
  • • 802.11g: OFDM at 2.4GHz
  • • 802.11b: DSSS/CCK
  • • 802.11a: First OFDM
